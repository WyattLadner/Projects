A birthday attack is a cryptographic attack that uses the Birthday Paradox to find collisions in hash functions.

The Birthday Paradox states that in a room of 23 people, there is about a 50% chance that two or more of them have the same birthday.

By this logic, a birthday attack inputs random inputs into a hashing algorithm until a collision is found, rather than trying every
possible input. This will significantly decrease the compution needed to break the algorithm.

This kind of attack can show the weakness of a given hash funciton. It is used in the real world against digital signatures and hash-
based authentication systems. Modern systems defend against these attacks by having longer outputs, like SHA256.

I will demonstrate this attack on the simpleHash.py I created.
